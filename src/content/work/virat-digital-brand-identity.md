## The brief

An identity for my own studio that holds up at every size, from a browser tab to a full-width hero, and that one person can apply consistently without starting from scratch each time.

## Approach

The mark is a single V, kept from the studio's first website and recoloured: a marigold tile with the letter in dark ink. Archivo carries the voice, set wide and heavy for headlines (*virat* means immense) and at normal width for reading.

Colour is one accent and two pairs of neutrals. Marigold marks the next thing to do; near-black and off-white carry the dark theme, ink and paper the light one. Each colour is defined once as a design token, so both themes draw on the same set. The 3D V on the home page was made in Adobe Firefly.
