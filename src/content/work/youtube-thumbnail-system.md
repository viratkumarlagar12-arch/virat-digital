## The brief

Thumbnails for my own channel, @viratdigitalmarketing, designed to be recognisable from one video to the next and readable at a glance on a phone.

## Approach

One template carries every upload: the presenter on one side, three or four words in heavy two-colour type, in English or Hindi, and icons for the tools the video covers.
