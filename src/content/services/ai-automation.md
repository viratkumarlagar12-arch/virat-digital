## Start with the work, not the tool

The most useful automations are unglamorous: an enquiry copied from a form to a sheet to WhatsApp, a weekly report rebuilt by hand, one caption rewritten for three platforms. I map how the task is done today, then automate what actually costs you time.

## AI where it helps, people where it matters

AI drafts, summarises, sorts and extracts well, but it shouldn't be responsible for the result. Drafts arrive for your approval, summaries link to their source, and anything unusual is flagged.

## Small, reliable steps

Small automations that each do one job: easier to trust, easier to change, easy to switch off.
