## Fewer channels, done properly

Small brands rarely fail by picking the wrong platform. They fail by trying to be everywhere. A good plan picks one or two channels where your audience already is, at a rhythm you can keep for months.

## Being found when people search

Search is where people go when they're ready to act. I check what your audience searches for and how your site and Google Business Profile show up, then make both clearer.

## Content with a reason

Every post has a job: be found, show your work, answer a question, or prompt a message. Themes follow those jobs, so you always know what to make next.
