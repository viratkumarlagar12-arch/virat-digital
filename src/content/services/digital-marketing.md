## Fewer channels, done properly

Most small brands don't fail at marketing because they picked the wrong platform. They fail because they try to be everywhere and run out of time. A good plan picks the one or two channels where your audience already is, and a posting rhythm you can keep up for months, not weeks.

## Being found when people search

Search is where people go when they're ready to act: "logo designer near me", "how to grow a YouTube channel", a business's name before they visit. I look at what your audience actually searches for, how your website and Google Business Profile show up today, and what would make them clearer and more complete.

## Content with a reason

Each post or video in the plan has a job: to be found, to show your work, to answer a common question, or to give people a reason to get in touch. Content themes are built around those jobs, so you always know what to make next and why.
