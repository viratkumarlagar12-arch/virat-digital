## Designed for the size people actually see

YouTube shows one thumbnail at many sizes, and most viewers browse on phones. So I design for the smallest first: one clear subject, a few words at most, strong contrast, nothing under the duration stamp.

I run my own channel, [@viratdigitalmarketing](https://youtube.com/@viratdigitalmarketing), so I design with YouTube's crops and overlays in mind.

## A look viewers recognise

Thumbnails work best as a system: the same type, colour and layout for each kind of video. Related, never identical.

## Banners that survive every crop

A banner is cropped differently on TV, desktop and phone. Your name and key message sit in the area visible everywhere; the rest crops gracefully.
