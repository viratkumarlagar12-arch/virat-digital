## What a brand has to do

Your customers meet you on a signboard, a WhatsApp profile, an invoice, an Instagram post. A brand identity makes them all look like the same business. Without one, every graphic starts from scratch.

## How I approach it

I start with questions, not sketches: who you sell to, who you compete with, where your logo will be seen. Each direction is tested at real sizes and in one colour before it's refined.

Then I build what you'll reuse daily: colours that hold up in print and on screen, typefaces you can install, and a short guide anyone can follow.

## Social media graphics

A few templates for the posts you make most, built on your brand. You get finished posts, plus templates for Canva or Photoshop.
