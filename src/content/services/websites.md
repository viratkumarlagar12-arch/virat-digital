## What a small business website has to do

For most small businesses a website has one job: help someone who has just heard of you decide to get in touch. That person is usually on a phone, often on a slow connection, and gives a page a few seconds. So I design mobile first, keep pages light so they load quickly, and put the way to contact you, whether a call, WhatsApp or a short form, where it's always easy to reach.

## Built to be found

Search engines reward the same things visitors do: fast pages, clear headings and content that answers real questions. Every site gets the technical groundwork: a sensible page structure, titles and descriptions for each page, a sitemap, and structured data that tells Google what your business is and where it works.
