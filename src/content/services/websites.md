## What a small business website has to do

One job: help someone who just heard of you decide to get in touch. They're on a phone, often on a slow connection, and give you seconds. So pages are light, built mobile first, and calling, WhatsApp or the form is always one tap away.

## Built to be found

Search engines reward what visitors do: fast pages, clear headings, useful answers. Every site gets the groundwork: clean structure, page titles and descriptions, a sitemap and structured data.
